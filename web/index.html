A filter for a portfolio manager's day. It reads every exchange filing and end-of-day print across the book, and shows only what moved the needle.

Brief is the push side: a windowed digest ranked by materiality. Swipe a card right when you're done, left to mute that kind of filing for that stock.

Watch is where you decide what "unusual" means. Drag a threshold and see how often it would have pinged you.

Research is the pull side: promises from the last four earnings calls checked against what was reported.

Ask is a conversation with your book: type or speak, and every answer links to the filing, price or research it came from.

Best on a phone. Open this page there, or narrow the window.
